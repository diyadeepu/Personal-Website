## 💡 Pro Tips

### Before Launch:
1. Proofread everything (typos hurt credibility)
2. Test all links (contact, social, navigation)
3. Test on actual phone (not just browser DevTools)
4. Get external feedback (fresh eyes catch issues)
5. Set up analytics (understand your audience)

### After Launch:
1. Share on LinkedIn (make post, not just link)
2. Add to resume/email signature
3. Send to mentors/advisors
4. Monitor for broken links (quarterly)
5. Update with new achievements (semester basis)

### For Maximum Impact:
1. Quantify everything (35% not "faster")
2. Show your thinking (not just results)
3. Make it easy to contact you (calendar link + email)
4. Update regularly (shows you're active)
5. Use specific language (AI PM not "tech PM")
