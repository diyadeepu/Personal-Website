## 🎯 Case Interview Prep

### Framework to Master: CIRCLES Method
**C**omprehend
**I**dentify
**R**esearch
**C**hoose metrics
**L**everage insights
**E**valuate
**S**upport with data

### Common PM Case Questions

1. **Product Design:** "Design an AI product for X problem"
2. **Metrics:** "How would you measure success for Y?"
3. **Strategy:** "Should company enter market Z?"
4. **User Research:** "How would you understand user needs?"
5. **Hardware-Software:** "Design a smart home device" (your advantage!)

### Practice Strategy
- Do 10-15 cases before interviews
- Record yourself (cringe but effective)
- Practice with friends, mentors
- Reference framework, don't memorize
- Focus on thinking process, not perfect answer

### Your Unique Angle
In cases, mention:
- Hardware-software tradeoffs
- Integration complexity
- Real-world constraints (from MIME experience)
- This sets you apart from pure software PMs
